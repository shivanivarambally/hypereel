- Clarified code comments and documentation: discovery-only `confirmed` is selection eligibility, not independent verification. Earlier curated tests do not prove that verification is generally worthless.

## Follow-up findings (not silently changed in this publication)

1. **Detection taxonomy and rules prompt disagree.** `rules_prompt_text()` asks for one label for a steal/turnover possession change, while the full source benchmark counts both annotations. Decide whether the product scores possessions or statistical events and align the prompt/scorer explicitly. Historical scores must retain their original policy.
2. **Rules checks depend on incomplete detections.** A missing preceding shot in a short window may reflect clipping or model omission, not an illegal rebound. Unknown possession boundaries and unnormalized team aliases can cause incorrect rules routing. Treat this as review assistance, not a referee or ground-truth validator.
3. **Transition observability is incomplete.** `last_run_transitions` is module-global rather than run-scoped; phase-1 confidence is currently null. Because transitions are built after rules routing changes the decision to potential, `rules_blocked` is not reliably distinguished from other review routing. Do not claim these counters independently establish verifier quality.
4. **Discovery-only uncertainty has a compatibility compromise.** All discovered labels become selection-eligible; low-confidence filtering and human approval remain essential. An explicit unverified status would be clearer than overloading `confirmed`.
5. **Full-game readiness remains unproven.** Cross-window duplicate events, all-event narrative summaries, made-basket recall, rebound attribution and paired verification on the broader sweep remain unfinished. The third-game holdout stays sealed.

The pending runtime behavior was preserved rather than changing prompts or routing without a fresh measured experiment. These limitations are not resolved by passing unit tests.
